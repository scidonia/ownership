## Checks that need the sibling checkout

`scripts/check_artifacts.sh` and `scripts/check_runtime_binding.sh` both require
`../lean-examples` at the pinned revision; the artifact gate fails loudly if the
sibling is at another revision or dirty. All Lean builds, the axiom audit, the
claim census and the paper build need only this repository and the dev shell.

## Scope of the evidence

The checks above establish kernel acceptance, the axiom footprint, that every
displayed Lean name resolves, and that the paper builds and reads correctly. They
do not establish that a paper sentence means what its declaration says — that is
the claim map's human-reviewed column — and they do not turn any of the following
into claims: C semantics, replay-to-backend identity, address identity, free and
cascade, physical reference counts, transport of accounting to runtime objects, a
step-by-step agreement between the counted and abstract interpreters inside the
pair-live window, the emitted recursive callee or continuation, or data-dependent
recursion depth.
