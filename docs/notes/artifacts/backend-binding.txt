## Generated C: Scidonia/FracPerm/StateVar/Layout5.c

The fused path update (`setAt___redArg`) and the take-out:

- `4102` `lp_..._setAt___redArg(...)`: the declaration's entry; the `Path.index`
  arm follows the `switch`.
- `4122`, `4171` `!lean_is_exclusive(v_x_1223_)`: the pair-reuse tests for the
  two container arms.
- `4235` `lp_..._assertExclusiveArrayImpl___redArg(...)`: the array check.
- `4244` `lp_..._assertExclusiveImpl___redArg(...)`: the child check on `snd`.
- `4245` `lp_..._setAt___redArg(v_p_1248_, v_x_1222_, v___x_1258_)`: the
  recursive call on the checked child.
- `4246` `v_xs_x27_1260_ = lean_array_fset(v_fst_1255_, v_i_1247_, v___x_1259_)`:
  the put-back into the extracted parent at the selected index with the
  recursive result.
- `2089` `lp_..._takeOut___redArg`: `2093` `lean_array_fget`, `2094`
  `lean_array_fset`, `2095` `lean_alloc_ctor(0, 2, 0)` with the two

## Runtime header: include/lean/lean.h

- `759` `static inline bool lean_is_exclusive(lean_object * o)`; `767`
  `lean_is_exclusive_obj`.
- `lean_array_set_core` tests exclusivity before the in-place store;
  `lean_array_fset` (called by the generated code) reaches it.
- `lean_array_uset` is the update primitive the model's `arraySet` names.

## IR-shape gate

`ScidoniaTest/IRShape.lean` reads the post-RC IR of `Layout5.setAt`, its
`Path.index` alternative, `takeOut`, and `Structured.execP`/`execR` through
`IR.findEnvDecl` (names at :284-300; survey entries at :394-399; requirements at
:490-540) and fails the build when the constructs or their order do not hold.
The gate runs `lake build ScidoniaTest`.

## Linked probe modes (gate output)

Check off: `separate-exact`, `separate-spare`, `inline-io`, `sealed`,
`inline-exact`, `closed-case` all exit 0.
Check on: `separate-exact`, `inline-io`, `sealed` exit 0; `inline-exact` and
`closed-case` exit 1 (the armed check aborts on the copy).

## What is bound, and what remains trusted

Bound: the pinned revision and clean tree, the artifact captures, the
post-reference-counting IR shapes, the generated C's check/recursive/put-back
calls and the runtime header's primitives, and the linked probe's statuses for
the certified and control shapes.

Not bound: the C semantics itself. The runtime model's `isExclusive` and
`arraySet` are the documented meanings of `lean_is_exclusive` and
`lean_array_uset`; the gate shows the primitives are present and the observed
behaviour agrees, not that the model refines the linked runtime. Replay-to-
backend identity (A10), address identity and free/cascade remain assumptions.

## Differential corpus: the model's predicates against the runtime

`runtime-bridge` mode `differential`, check off (the copy cases would abort under
the armed check; the other modes cover that):

| Case | Predicted | Observed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| unique array | exclusive true, in place | agree |
| live alias (count two) | exclusive false, copy, alias keeps 0 | agree |
| alias released before the write | exclusive true, in place | agree |
| persistent literal | exclusive false, copy | agree |
| closure holds the array | exclusive false, copy | agree |

The model's predicate is count one and not persistent; the corpus constructs
those states and compares them with `isExclusiveUnsafe` and the address change.
This is validation, not proof: the corpus is finite, and the C semantics of the
primitives is still the trust boundary.
